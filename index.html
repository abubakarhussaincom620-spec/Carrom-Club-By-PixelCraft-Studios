Before You Email Us

Common questions

Is the money in Stakes Match real?+
No. Every coin in Carrom Club is a virtual, in-game currency earned by playing. Nothing can be withdrawn or exchanged for real money, and no purchase is required to play any mode.
My striker looks stuck in the wrong place mid-match.+
This usually clears up if both players fully close and reopen the app before the next match. If it keeps happening, email us with which player (the one who created the room, or the one who joined) it happened to.
Online matchmaking says "waiting for opponent" forever.+
Quick Match and Stakes Match need another real player searching the same stake tier at the same time — it won't pair you with a bot. Try Play Online with a room code instead if you're testing solo with a friend.
The app won't install — "App Not Installed".+
Uninstall any previous version first, make sure "Install unknown apps" is allowed for whichever app you opened the file from, and avoid transferring the APK over WhatsApp (it can corrupt the file). A fresh transfer via Drive or USB fixes this almost every time.
I lost coins from a match that disconnected.+
If a match ends because your opponent disconnects mid-game, your entry fee is refunded automatically on your device. If that didn't happen for you, email us the approximate time and the stake tier.
The Fine Print

Privacy & data

What we store

Your level, XP, coin balance, and equipped striker are saved only on your own device. Carrom Club does not require an account and does not run its own game servers — online matches connect two players' devices to each other directly.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, phone number, or contacts.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.
  • In-game coins are never convertible to real money.

Advertising

Carrom Club may show ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve and measure ads, in line with Google's own advertising policies. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Contact

Questions about your data or this policy can go to support.carromclub@gmail.com.
